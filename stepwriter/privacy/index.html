StepWriter 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日 · 開發者：souraizuni · English version

StepWriter 是一款讓你把步數手動補登到 Health Connect 的 Android app。本政策說明 StepWriter 如何存取、使用、儲存與分享資料。簡單來說：你的資料不會離開你的裝置。

存取哪些資料

StepWriter 透過 Android 的 Health Connect 存取以下資料，且只在你授權後才會存取：

  • 步數（讀取）：讀取今天所有來源的步數紀錄，用來顯示 Health Connect 整合後的今日總數與各來源明細；並讀取 StepWriter 自己近 30 天寫入的紀錄，讓你查看與刪除。
  • 步數（寫入）：把你輸入的步數寫入 Health Connect。所有寫入的紀錄都標記為「手動輸入」。

StepWriter 不存取步數以外的任何健康資料，也不存取你的聯絡人、位置、相片或其他個人資料。

如何使用資料

讀取的步數只用於在 app 畫面上顯示給你看，以及讓你管理自己補登的紀錄。不用於廣告、分析、信用評估或任何其他用途。

資料儲存

步數資料存放在你裝置上的 Health Connect 中，由 Android 系統管理。StepWriter 本身不另外保存任何健康資料的副本。

資料分享

StepWriter 不會將任何資料上傳、分享、出售或轉移給開發者或任何第三方。App 沒有網路存取權限，也沒有內建任何廣告、分析或追蹤工具。

你寫入 Health Connect 的步數，會依你在 Health Connect 中的設定，被你自行授權的其他 app 讀取。這部分由 Health Connect 與那些 app 的政策管理。

Health Connect 有限使用聲明

StepWriter 對 Health Connect 資料的存取與使用，遵守 Google Play 的 Android 健康權限相關規定（參見 Android Health Permissions: Guidance and FAQs），包括其中的有限使用（Limited Use）規定。

你的控制權

  • 你可以隨時在 Health Connect 設定中撤銷 StepWriter 的讀取或寫入權限。
  • 你可以在 StepWriter 內刪除單筆或全部由 StepWriter 寫入的紀錄，也可以在 Health Connect 中刪除。
  • 移除 StepWriter 不會自動刪除它已寫入 Health Connect 的紀錄；如需刪除，請在移除前使用 app 內的「刪除全部」，或到 Health Connect 中刪除。

兒童

StepWriter 並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象。

政策變更

若本政策有變更，會更新此頁面並修改上方的生效日期。若日後 app 的資料處理方式有實質改變（例如加入網路功能），會在新版 app 中另行告知。

StepWriter Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Developer: souraizuni

StepWriter is an Android app that lets you manually log steps to Health Connect. This policy explains how StepWriter accesses, uses, stores and shares data. In short: your data never leaves your device.

Data accessed

StepWriter accesses the following data through Android Health Connect, only after you grant permission:

  • Steps (read): today's step records from all sources, to show Health Connect's combined daily total and a per-source breakdown; and the records StepWriter itself wrote in the last 30 days, so you can review and delete them.
  • Steps (write): the step counts you enter are written to Health Connect. Every record written is marked as a manual entry.

StepWriter does not access any health data other than steps, nor your contacts, location, photos or other personal data.

How data is used

Step data is used only to display it to you within the app and to let you manage the records you logged. It is not used for advertising, analytics, credit assessment or any other purpose.

Storage

Step data is stored in Health Connect on your device and managed by Android. StepWriter does not keep its own copy of any health data.

Sharing

StepWriter does not upload, share, sell or transfer any data to the developer or any third party. The app has no internet permission and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Steps you write to Health Connect may be read by other apps you have authorized in Health Connect, under Health Connect's and those apps' own policies.

Health Connect Limited Use

StepWriter's access to and use of information received from Health Connect adheres to Google Play's requirements for Android health permissions (see Android Health Permissions: Guidance and FAQs), including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • You can revoke StepWriter's read or write access at any time in Health Connect settings.
  • You can delete one or all records written by StepWriter inside the app, or in Health Connect.
  • Uninstalling StepWriter does not automatically delete records it wrote to Health Connect. To remove them, use "Delete all" in the app before uninstalling, or delete them in Health Connect.

Children

StepWriter is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If the app's data handling changes materially (for example, adding network features), you will be notified in the updated app.
